# Release notes

Updates arrive by themselves through the Chrome Web Store. Your version is shown on `chrome://extensions`, next to the extension's name.

## 1.0.14 · 21 November 2026

- **My Library**: everything you saved in one gallery, with collections, favourites, tags, duplicates, two pictures side by side and a timed slideshow
- **Keep a profile up to date**: once a day the extension checks the profiles you chose and brings in their new posts
- **Smart search** and **Google Drive backup** in My Library, on paid plans. Smart search runs on your own computer.
- New profiles are saved under `Downloads\Instagram\<profile>`. A profile you downloaded before keeps the folder it already has.

## 1.0.12 · 10 November 2026

- **Saved posts**: open Saved on your own profile and download all of them, one collection, or only the ones you choose. Your 20 most recent are free.
- Name each profile's folder after its username, its profile name, or both

## Earlier versions · September and October 2026

- Fixes after Instagram changed its website, so profiles load again (1.0.8 to 1.0.10)
- The panel can be minimised to a small round button, and remembers it (1.0.5)
- First release (1.0.0)
